# Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences
## Examination Regulations, Registration & Hall Ticket Guidelines
**Document ID:** AITS-EXAM-REG-022  
**Governing Body:** Office of the Controller of Examinations (CoE)  
**Applicability:** All Regular and Arrear / Supplementary University Examinations

---

### 1. General Overview
All mid-term assessments and End-Semester Examinations (ESE) are administered by the Office of the Controller of Examinations. Students must adhere strictly to the academic calendar deadlines and examination conduct regulations.

---

### 2. Examination Registration Procedure
1. **Eligibility Check:**
   - Minimum cumulative attendance of 75% in each registered theory and laboratory course.
   - Satisfactory internal continuous assessment (CIA) completion.
   - Absolute clearance of tuition and hostel fees for the semester.
2. **Online Registration Steps:**
   - Go to ERP Portal > **Examinations > Exam Registration Form**.
   - Review pre-populated list of core and elective courses.
   - In case of supplementary/backlog papers, select the relevant course codes carefully.
   - Verify student details (name spelling, register number, exam session).
   - Click "Submit and Proceed to Exam Fee Checkout" (if applicable for arrears). Regular semester exam fee is included in the semester tuition package.
   - Download the provisional exam registration confirmation slip.
3. **Registration Deadlines:**
   - Regular registration window: Opens 4 weeks before commencement of exams and closes 2 weeks prior.
   - Late registration with nominal penalty (₹500): Available up to 7 days before examination commencement.
   - Strict closure: No registrations accepted within 6 days of examinations under any circumstances.

---

### 3. Fee Clearance & Mandatory Approvals
- **Finance Section Clearance:** An automated lock is placed on exam registration and hall ticket issuance for students with fee dues exceeding ₹0.
- **Library & Laboratory Dues:** Overdue books and unreturned lab consumables must be cleared before the exam registration deadline.
- **Conditional Admittance:** Under severe financial distress, students can apply for a deferred payment waiver via the Dean of Student Welfare (DSW) at least 10 days before exams.

---

### 4. Hall Ticket Issuance & Required Documentation
1. **Release Schedule:**
   - Digital Hall Tickets (Admit Cards) are generated 5 calendar days before the examination start date.
2. **Steps to Download:**
   - Login to ERP > **Examinations > Download Hall Ticket / Admit Card**.
   - Verify exam slots, course codes, hall number, and photograph clarity.
   - Print a clear color or high-contrast monochrome copy on standard A4 paper.
3. **Mandatory Documentation for Exam Entry:**
   - **Official Printed Hall Ticket** signed by the student.
   - **Original College Student Smart ID Card** (Laminated or plastic card).
   - *Alternative in case of lost ID Card:* Temporary Duplicate ID receipt stamped by the Chief Proctor / Registrar + Government ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Driving License).

---

### 5. Examination Hall Protocols & Malpractice Rules
- **Reporting Time:** Students must occupy their assigned desks 20 minutes prior to exam commencement. No entry is permitted 15 minutes after exam start time.
- **Permitted Items:** Non-programmable scientific calculator (models FX-82, FX-991ES or approved series only), standard blue/black pens, pencils, eraser, and transparent water bottle.
- **Prohibited Items:** Smart watches, mobile phones, fitness bands, programmable calculators, scratch pads, and unauthorized study notes.
- **Malpractice Consequences:**
  - Category 1 (Possession of unauthorized material/notes): Cancellation of the current paper.
  - Category 2 (Copying / Passing material / Electronic device usage): Cancellation of all examinations in the current semester and possible 1-year suspension.
  - Category 3 (Impersonation): Immediate expulsion and filing of formal police report.

---

### 6. Supplementary / Revaluation Guidelines
- **Answer Script Scrutiny / Photocopy:** Students may request answer book photocopies within 7 days of result declaration for ₹300 per paper.
- **Revaluation Fee:** ₹750 per subject. If the change in marks is greater than 15%, 50% of the revaluation fee is refunded.
- **Query Desk:** Office of the CoE, Examination Wing, 2nd Floor, Exam Control Block. Email: `coe-queries@aits.edu.in`.
