# Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences
## Official Policy: Bonafide Certificate Issuance & Guidelines
**Document ID:** AITS-ADM-POL-014  
**Effective Date:** Academic Year 2024-2025 onwards  
**Office In-Charge:** Student Affairs & Academic Records Office (Room 102, Admin Block)

---

### 1. Purpose & Eligibility
A Bonafide Certificate is an official college document certifying that a student is currently enrolled as a bona fide regular student in a specific degree program, department, and academic year.
- **Eligible Students:** All actively enrolled Undergraduate (B.Tech), Postgraduate (M.Tech, MBA, MCA), and Doctoral (Ph.D.) students without pending disciplinary suspension.
- **Common Purposes:**
  1. Passport, Visa, and Police Verification applications.
  2. National and State Government Scholarship applications.
  3. Student Concession passes (Metro, State Road Transport, Indian Railways).
  4. Educational Loan processing and bank verification.
  5. Internship and external academic project applications.

---

### 2. Application Procedure & Steps

Students may apply for a Bonafide Certificate via either the **Online ERP Portal** or **Physical Counter Application**:

#### Step 1: Online Application (Recommended)
1. Log in to the Student ERP Portal (`https://erp.aits.edu.in`) using your Student Roll Number and SSO credentials.
2. Navigate to **Student Services > Certificates > Request Bonafide Certificate**.
3. Select the certificate category (e.g., General, Visa/Passport, Bank Loan, Scholarship, Concession).
4. Specify the organization/authority to which the certificate is addressed.
5. Upload supporting documents if requested (e.g., passport application receipt, scholarship notification form).
6. Submit the request and note down the **Service Request Tracking ID (SR-ID)**.

#### Step 2: Physical / Offline Application
1. Download Form A-12 (Bonafide Application Form) from the college portal downloads section or collect it from Counter 2, Student Records Office.
2. Fill in all details in block letters (Name as in High School Certificate, Roll Number, Branch, Semester, Section).
3. Obtain the signature and endorsement from your Department Faculty Mentor / Head of Department (HOD).
4. Submit the signed form at Counter 2 between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM on working days.

---

### 3. Required Identification Documents
Applicants must furnish or upload the following valid identification documents:
- **Mandatory:** Official College Student ID Card (Physical Smart Card or ERP digital ID).
- **Secondary Proof (any one):**
  - Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  - Previous semester grade card / Fee receipt for the current academic session.
- **For Visa / Passport Bonafide:** Copy of Passport Application Appointment receipt showing applicant details.
- **For Bank Loan Bonafide:** Formal loan estimate requisition letter issued by the lending bank.

---

### 4. Fee & Payment Terms
- **Standard Bonafide Certificate:** Free of charge for up to 3 requests per semester.
- **Additional Copies:** ₹50 per extra copy, payable via ERP payment gateway or Accounts Section Counter.
- **Urgent / Tatkal Processing:** ₹150 processing fee for emergency same-day dispatch.

---

### 5. Issuance Timelines & Delivery
- **Standard Processing Time:** 2 working days from the date of submission and department verification.
- **Urgent / Tatkal Processing Time:** Dispatched within 4 to 6 business hours (subject to HOD approval submitted before 12:00 PM).
- **Collection Mode:**
  - **Digitally Signed PDF:** Available for instant download via the ERP portal under "My Issued Documents" with an embedded verifiable QR code.
  - **Hard Copy with Institutional Seal:** Can be collected from Counter 2, Admin Block between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM upon presenting the original Student ID card.

---
