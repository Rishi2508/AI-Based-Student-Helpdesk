### 2. Membership & Borrowing Entitlements

Every student enrolled at the institute is automatically granted library borrowing privileges encoded into their RFID-enabled Student Smart Card.

| Student Category | Max Books Allowed | Loan Duration (Days) | Grace Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Undergraduate (B.Tech)** | 4 Books | 14 Days | 1 Day |
| **Postgraduate (M.Tech / MBA / MCA)** | 6 Books | 21 Days | 1 Day |
| **Ph.D. / Research Scholars** | 8 Books | 30 Days | 2 Days |
| **Book Bank (SC/ST & Merit-cum-Means)** | 4 Textbook Sets | Full Semester | End of Exams |

*Reference books, rare manuscripts, single-copy encyclopedias, and current issues of printed journals cannot be borrowed for home lending and must be consulted inside the Reference Reading Room only.*

### 3. Book Borrowing & Self-Checkout Protocol
1. **Self-Service Kiosk:**
   - Scan your Student Smart ID at the RFID Self-Checkout Station.
   - Place up to 4 books simultaneously on the RFID reader plate.
   - Confirm the transaction on screen and print the receipt showing the due dates.
2. **Assisted Circulation Counter:**
   - Present your physical Student ID and books at Circulation Counters 1 or 2.
   - Third-party borrowing or lending someone else's ID card is strictly forbidden and may lead to a 30-day suspension of borrowing privileges.

---

### 4. Book Renewal Procedures
Books may be renewed provided there are no active hold/reservation requests placed by other patrons:
- **Online Renewal (ERP / OPAC):**
  - Visit the library OPAC portal (`https://libopac.aits.edu.in`) or ERP > **Library Services**.
  - Login with student credentials and select **Active Loans > Renew**.
  - Online renewal can be performed up to **2 consecutive times** per book before physical verification is required.
  - Renewal must be requested at least 24 hours prior to the original due date.
- **Physical Renewal:**
  - Bring the book to the Circulation Desk for physical inspection by library staff to renew for an additional loan cycle.

---

### 5. Overdue Fines & Lost Book Policies
To encourage equitable access to learning resources, fines are assessed for overdue materials:

- **Standard Overdue Fine Structure:**
  - Days 1 to 7 overdue: ₹5 per book per day.
  - Days 8 to 21 overdue: ₹15 per book per day.
  - Beyond 21 days: ₹25 per book per day + automatic account freeze until clearance.
- **Payment of Fines:**
  - Fines can be paid directly at the library accounts terminal via UPI/Smart Card tap, or cleared via the ERP dues module.
- **Lost or Damaged Book Rules:**
  - If a student loses or damages a book, they must report it immediately to avoid accumulating overdue fines.
  - **Resolution Options:**
    1. Replace the lost book with a brand new copy of the same or latest edition + ₹100 catalog processing charge.
    2. Pay double the current retail replacement cost of the book (or 3x cost if out-of-print) + ₹200 handling fee.
