# Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences
## Attendance Rules, Minimum Requirements & Medical Condonation Policy
**Document ID:** AITS-ACAD-ATT-008  
**Applicability:** All Undergraduate and Postgraduate Degree Programs  
**Monitoring Body:** Academic Monitoring Cell & Department Attendance Committees

---

### 1. General Attendance Mandate
Consistent classroom attendance and laboratory participation are critical components of the academic learning process.
- **Minimum Required Attendance:** **75%** of the total contact hours conducted in each course (both lecture, tutorial, and practical components) during the semester.
- **Automatic Disqualification (Debarred / Detained):** Students securing less than 75% attendance in any course will NOT be permitted to write the End-Semester Examination for that course and will be awarded an 'FA' (Failed due to Attendance / Detained) grade.
- **Biometric / ERP Tracking:** Attendance is recorded by course instructors at the beginning of each lecture hour and updated on the ERP portal by 5:00 PM daily.

---

### 2. Attendance Slabs & Consequences

| Attendance Range | Eligibility Status | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **85% - 100%** | Full Eligibility | Eligible for Dean’s Honor List and academic merit scholarships |
| **75% - 84%** | Regular Eligibility | Normal permission to sit for End-Semester Examinations |
| **65% - 74%** | Conditional Eligibility | Eligible ONLY with Medical Condonation approval or Institutional Representation Approval, subject to payment of Condonation Fee |
| **Below 65%** | Ineligible / Debarred | Strictly debarred from writing final exams for the subject. Must re-register for the course during summer term or subsequent semester |

---

### 3. Medical Leave & Attendance Condonation

Students whose attendance falls between **65% and 74%** due to genuine illness, hospitalization, or bereavement in the immediate family may apply for attendance condonation under the following conditions:

#### Eligibility Criteria for Medical Condonation:
- Illness requiring prolonged bed rest (more than 3 consecutive working days) or hospitalization.
- Communicable diseases (COVID-19, chickenpox, dengue, malaria, typhoid, etc.).
- Major surgical procedures or accidental trauma.

#### Application Procedure for Medical Leave:
1. **Intimation:** Student or parent must send an email to the Class Advisor / Faculty Mentor within 48 hours of falling ill.
2. **Medical Certificate Submission:**
   - Must be issued by a registered medical practitioner (holding MBBS degree or higher) with registration number and clinical seal.
   - For private clinic treatment, the certificate must be countersigned by the Chief Medical Officer (CMO) at the Institute Health Centre.
   - For hospital admissions, discharge summary, diagnostic lab reports, and pharmacy receipts must be attached.
3. **Submission Deadline:** Complete medical documentation must be uploaded on ERP and physical originals submitted to the Department Office within **5 working days** of resuming classes. Late submissions will be rejected without appeal.

#### Condonation Fees & Approvals:
- **Condonation Application Processing Fee:** ₹1,000 per subject or ₹2,500 for whole semester condonation.
- **Approval Hierarchy:** Recommended by HOD -> Reviewed by Institute Medical Officer -> Approved by Dean (Academic Affairs).

---

### 4. Institutional Representation / On-Duty (OD) Leave
Students participating in sanctioned university activities are granted **On-Duty (OD) Attendance** credit:
- **Eligible Activities:**
  1. Inter-university sports tournaments representing the institute.
  2. National / International paper presentations, hackathons, and robotic competitions approved by Dean (R&D).
  3. Official NSS, NCC, or Youth Red Cross camps.
  4. Placement drives and interviews conducted by the Training & Placement Cell.
- **Maximum Permissible OD Days:** 12 working days per academic semester.
- **Procedure:** Prior written sanction from the faculty coordinator and HOD is mandatory at least 3 days before the scheduled event. Retrospective OD requests will not be entertained.

---

### 5. Grievance & Discrepancy Reporting
- Students must monitor their weekly attendance on the ERP portal.
- If an attendance discrepancy occurs (e.g., marked absent while present), the student must raise an online discrepancy ticket within **3 calendar days** of the lecture date. The course instructor will review the sign-in sheet and correct the log.
- Queries can be directed to the Department Academic Coordinator or `attendance@aits.edu.in`.
