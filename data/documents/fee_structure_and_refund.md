### 2. Approved Payment Methods & Channels

To ensure security and automated reconciliation, all fees must be remitted through official university banking channels:

1. **Online Payment Gateway (ERP Portal - Preferred):**
   - Login to ERP > **Finance > Fee Payment Gateway**.
   - Supports: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM), Net Banking (all major national/private banks), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay).
   - Zero convenience fee for UPI transactions; nominal bank switch charge applies to corporate credit cards.
2. **NEFT / RTGS Bank Transfer (Direct Virtual Account):**
   - Each student is assigned a unique Virtual Account Number (VAN) formatted as: `AITS<Student_Roll_Number>`.
   - Bank Name: State Bank of India (SBI)
   - Branch: Apex Campus Branch, Tech Park Road
   - IFSC Code: `SBIN0014299`
   - Account Name: Apex Institute of Technology Fee Account
   - *Important:* Always mention the student's Roll Number in the NEFT transaction remarks.
3. **Demand Draft (DD):**
   - DD drawn in favor of **"Apex Institute of Technology"**, payable at the city campus branch.
   - Student's name, roll number, and department must be legibly written on the back of the DD.
   - Hand-deliver DD to Accounts Desk 1, Admin Ground Floor.
4. **Cash Transactions:**
   - In accordance with Central Financial Regulations, cash payments exceeding ₹5,000 are **strictly prohibited** at college counters.

---

### 3. Payment Deadlines & Late Surcharge
- **Odd Semester Dues:** To be cleared on or before July 31st.
- **Even Semester Dues:** To be cleared on or before January 15th.
- **Grace Period:** 7 calendar days after the deadline without penalty.
- **Late Fee Structure:**
  - Day 8 to 20 past deadline: Flat ₹1,000 late surcharge.
  - Day 21 to 35 past deadline: ₹3,000 late surcharge.
  - Beyond 35 days: ERP student portal access will be temporarily locked, preventing attendance logging, course material downloads, and exam hall ticket release.

---

### 4. Fee Receipt Generation & Tax Invoices
1. **Automated Receipt Generation:**
   - Upon successful payment via the ERP gateway, the official tax invoice and electronic receipt (Form A-8) is generated immediately.
   - Students can download the digitally signed PDF under **Finance > Payment History & Receipts**.
2. **NEFT / DD Reconciliation:**
   - Payments made via NEFT or DD take 2 to 3 banking days for account reconciliation.
   - Once verified by the finance manager, receipt notification is dispatched via SMS and registered email.
3. **Education Loan / Income Tax (Section 80E) Certificates:**
   - Dedicated annual tuition fee breakdown certificates for parent tax rebates (80E) are downloadable from the ERP tax documents section.

---

### 5. Institutional Refund Policy (Withdrawal of Admission)

In alignment with national statutory regulatory guidelines, refund entitlements for students formally withdrawing from their program are calculated as follows:

| Point of Notice of Withdrawal | Percentage of Eligible Fee Refunded | Deduction Applicable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **15 days or more before** officially notified last date of admission | **100%** of tuition & institutional fees | Deduct processing fee of ₹1,000 (maximum) |
| **Less than 15 days before** officially notified last date of admission | **90%** of tuition & institutional fees | 10% deduction of total semester fee |
| **Less than 15 days after** officially notified last date of admission | **80%** of tuition & institutional fees | 20% deduction of total semester fee |
| **Between 16 and 30 days after** formally announced last date | **50%** of tuition & institutional fees | 50% deduction of total semester fee |
| **More than 30 days after** formally announced last date | **0% (No refund)** | Only refundable Caution Deposit will be returned |

#### Refund Processing Timeline & Procedure:
- Submit formal withdrawal Form W-1 to the Admissions & Registrar's Office.
- Attach original fee receipts and canceled bank cheque showing student or parent account number and IFSC code.
- Approved refunds are credited directly to the nominated bank account within **15 to 20 working days**.
