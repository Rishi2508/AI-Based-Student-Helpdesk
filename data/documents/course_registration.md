# Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences
## Academic Course Registration & Credit Regulations
**Document ID:** AITS-ACAD-REG-003  
**Applicability:** Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) for all UG and PG Programs  
**Authority:** Office of the Dean (Academic Affairs)

---

### 1. Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) Overview
The Institute follows a flexible Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) allowing students to design their academic path through mandatory core subjects, professional electives, open multidisciplinary electives, and skill-enhancement courses.

---

### 2. Credit Limits per Semester

Students must maintain semester credit loads strictly within the prescribed boundary conditions:

| Category / Semester Level | Minimum Credits Required | Normal Recommended Load | Maximum Permissible Credits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Year (Semesters 1 & 2)** | 18 Credits | 22 Credits | 24 Credits |
| **Second Year (Semesters 3 & 4)** | 16 Credits | 22 Credits | 26 Credits |
| **Third Year (Semesters 5 & 6)** | 16 Credits | 24 Credits | 28 Credits (with CGPA >= 8.0) |
| **Final Year (Semesters 7 & 8)** | 12 Credits | 18 Credits | 24 Credits |
| **Academic Probation (CGPA < 5.0)** | 14 Credits | 16 Credits | 18 Credits (strictly capped) |

- **Minimum Credit Rule:** Registering for fewer than the minimum credits requires formal exemption signed by the Head of Department and Academic Dean, otherwise the student cannot be classified as full-time.
- **Credit Overload Privilege:** Students with a cumulative CGPA >= 8.5 and no active backlogs may register for an additional elective course (up to 4 extra credits above the max limit) for honors degree qualification.

---

### 3. Course Registration Schedule & Phases

Course registration is divided into three distinct operational windows on the ERP system:

#### Phase 1: Pre-Registration (Elective Bidding & Preference Submission)
- **Schedule:** 3 weeks before the conclusion of the preceding semester.
- **Action:** Students submit preferences for Professional Electives (PE) and Open Electives (OE).
- **Seat Allocation:** Seats are allocated through an automated algorithm factoring in merit CGPA and preference rank.

#### Phase 2: Regular Registration Window
- **Schedule:** Commences 7 calendar days before the first day of semester instruction and closes at 11:59 PM on Day 2 of instruction.
- **Action:** Finalize core course sections, lab batches, and confirm allocated elective seats.
- **Prerequisite Check:** ERP automatically verifies prerequisites; students who have not cleared mandatory prerequisite courses will have dependent registrations blocked.
- **Approval:** Requires digital sign-off from the assigned Faculty Advisor / Mentor.

#### Phase 3: Add / Drop Window
- **Schedule:** Opens on Day 3 of the semester and closes sharply on **Day 10 of instruction at 5:00 PM**.
- **Permissible Adjustments:**
  - Drop a registered elective without any academic transcript penalty (no 'W' withdrawal grade recorded).
  - Add an alternate elective course subject to seat availability and schedule conflict absence.
  - Swap lab or tutorial batches with mutual consent and faculty advisor approval.
- **Post Add/Drop Late Drop:**
  - Allowed up to Week 4 of instruction with an official 'W' (Withdrawn) mark on the grade sheet. No refunds of course-specific lab fees apply.

---

### 4. Step-by-Step Registration Procedure
1. Log in to ERP > **Academics > Semester Course Registration**.
2. Review the list of pre-assigned Mandatory Core Courses.
3. Select your preferred electives from the available elective buckets.
4. Verify there are **no timetable time-slot conflicts** displayed on the interactive schedule matrix.
5. Click **"Submit for Advisor Approval"**.
6. Attend the in-person or virtual consultation with your Faculty Advisor.
7. Upon advisor endorsement, click **"Final Submit & Lock Registration"**.
8. Print or save the signed Course Registration Card (Form C-1) for department record verification.

---

### 5. Audit Courses and MOOC (NPTEL / Coursera) Credit Transfers
- **Audit Courses:** Students may audit up to 1 course per semester without earning credits (graded as Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory).
- **Credit Transfer via NPTEL / SWAYAM:**
  - Up to 12 credits throughout the degree program can be earned via approved 8-week or 12-week NPTEL/Swayam MOOC courses.
  - Prior approval from the Department Academic Committee (DAC) must be obtained before enrolling in external online certifications.
