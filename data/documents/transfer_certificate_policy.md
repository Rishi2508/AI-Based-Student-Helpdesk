# Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences
## Transfer Certificate (TC) & Institutional Clearance Policy
**Document ID:** AITS-REG-TC-019  
**Office of Origin:** Office of the Registrar & Student Services  
**Target Audience:** Graduating Students, Withdrawing Candidates, and Inter-College Transfer Applicants

---

### 1. Purpose & Scope
A Transfer Certificate (TC), often issued concurrently with a Migration Certificate and Character/Conduct Certificate, certifies that a student has officially concluded or discontinued their studies at Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences and has fulfilled all institutional obligations.

---

### 2. Comprehensive "No-Dues" Clearance Procedure

Before a Transfer Certificate can be drafted and authorized, the applicant must obtain explicit electronic or physical clearance from all institutional stakeholders:

#### Required No-Dues Signoffs & Departments:
1. **Department Laboratory & Workshops:** Return of specialized project kits, borrowed microcontroller development boards, tools, and consumables.
2. **Central Library:** Complete return of all borrowed books, settlement of outstanding late penalty fines, and deactivation of RFID borrower privileges.
3. **Finance & Accounts Section:** Verification of complete settlement of tuition fees, bus transport fees, exam charges, and assessment of refundable Caution Deposit.
4. **Hostel & Mess Administration:** Vacation of hostel room, room inventory inspection, and settlement of mess rebate adjustments.
5. **Sports & Physical Education Department:** Return of borrowed sports gear, gym membership cards, and team jerseys.
6. **Training & Placement Cell (T&P):** Registration record closure and update of final employment/higher study placement status.
7. **National Service Scheme (NSS) / NCC / Youth Red Cross Units:** Clearance of equipment and attendance records (if enrolled).

---

### 3. Step-by-Step Application Workflow

#### Method 1: Automated Digital Clearance (ERP Portal)
1. **Initiate Clearance:** Login to ERP > **Student Services > Exit & Clearance > Request TC**.
2. **Select Reason:** Graduation / Degree Completion, Transfer to Another University, or Voluntary Discontinuation.
3. **Automated Routing:** The clearance request is automatically routed to all 7 departmental portals. Department heads review and approve signoffs within their dashboards.
4. **Status Tracking:** The student can monitor progress via a live progress bar (e.g., "5/7 Departments Cleared").
5. **Final Registrar Approval:** Once 100% signoffs are logged, the application reaches the Assistant Registrar for final clearance.

#### Method 2: Physical Clearance Form (Form TC-10)
- In cases where ERP credentials have lapsed or for legacy alumni:
  1. Download and print Form TC-10 ("Institutional Clearance & No-Dues Slip").
  2. Personally visit each designated office to obtain ink signatures and official departmental rubber stamps.
  3. Submit the completed, sealed sheet to Window 3, Registrar's Office.

---

### 4. Required Supporting Documents
When collecting or submitting the TC request, the applicant must provide:
- **Original Student Smart ID Card** (must be surrendered to the Registrar's Office for cancellation).
- **Provisional Degree Certificate or Final Grade Transcript** (for graduating students).
- **Admission Offer Letter / Migration Requisition** from the receiving university (for transfer cases).
- **Government Photo ID Proof** (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or PAN card).
- **Proof of Payment:** Bank transaction receipt for TC processing fee (if applicable).

---

### 5. Fees & Processing Timelines

| Category | Standard Processing Time | Express / Fast-Track Mode | Fee Applicable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Graduating Batch (Post-Convocation)** | 5 to 7 working days | 2 working days | Included in Convocation Fee |

  - **Registered Speed Post / Courier:** Available for outstation students upon payment of ₹150 postal charge.
  - **Direct University Dispatch:** TC and Migration can be dispatched in an officially sealed envelope directly to the Dean of Admissions of the recipient university.

---

### 6. Frequently Asked Questions
- **What happens to my Caution Deposit?**  
  Upon completion of the No-Dues signoff, the Finance Section automatically initiates the refund of the ₹15,000 Caution Deposit (minus any authorized deductions) to the bank account specified in your ERP profile within 21 days.
- **Can my parent or legal guardian collect my TC on my behalf?**  
  Yes, provided they produce a signed authorization letter from the student, the original Student ID card, and a valid government photo identity proof of the parent/guardian.
- **Contact:** Office of the Assistant Registrar (Certificates Wing), Ground Floor, Admin Block. Email: `registrar-tc@aits.edu.in`.
